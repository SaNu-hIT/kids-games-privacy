Privacy Policy – Tell the Time – Kids Clock

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Tell the Time is a game that teaches children to read an analog clock, made by ZerohTech. The game works fully offline. It does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data, from children or anyone else.

What stays on the device

The app saves only a few things on the device itself, so the game remembers where your child left off:

  • Your chosen language, stars, streak, which levels are open, and whether the one-time unlock was bought.

This information never leaves the device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

One-time purchase

The first levels are free. Grown-ups can buy a one-time unlock for the remaining levels. It sits behind a question for grown-ups, so children can't reach it by accident. There is no subscription.

The payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The app never sees card, UPI or bank details, and we receive no personal information about the buyer from the app. Google's handling of the payment is covered by the Google Play terms and Google's privacy policy.

Updates

The app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, so it can update itself. This check is done by Google Play and the app sends no personal data.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in or personal details asked for.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No internet access is used by the app itself.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.
  • No links out of the app.

Children

This app is designed for children. It collects no personal information from children (or anyone else), so nothing is ever shared with us or with third parties.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
