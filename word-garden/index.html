Privacy Policy – Word Garden: Words for Kids

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Word Garden is a word-learning game for children, made by ZerohTech. Children match pictures to words in English, Malayalam and Tamil. The app does not collect, upload, sell or share personal data from children or anyone else.

What stays on the device

The app saves only a few things on the device itself, so the garden remembers where your child left off:

  • Your chosen language, sound settings and which words have been learned.
  • Stars and stickers earned.

This information never leaves the device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Purchases

Some themes are free. Others unlock with a subscription (Kids Pass, monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan) or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play, which asks for payment details and manages your subscription. We never see or store your payment details. Purchases are behind a grown-up question inside the app. You can cancel a subscription any time in the Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions. To confirm a purchase, the app checks with Google Play which purchases your Google account owns; no other information is sent.

Voice

Words are read aloud by your device's built-in text-to-speech. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in or personal details asked for.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.
  • No links out of the app.

Children

This app is designed for children. It collects no personal information from children (or anyone else), so nothing is ever shared with us or with third parties.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
