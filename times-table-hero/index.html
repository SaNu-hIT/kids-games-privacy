Privacy Policy – Times Table Hero – Kids Tables

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Times Table Hero is a times tables practice game for children, made by ZerohTech. The game works fully offline. It does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data, from children or anyone else.

What stays on the device

The app saves only a few things on the device itself, so the game remembers where your child left off:

  • Your chosen language, stars, points, best streak, which levels are done, and whether the one-time unlock was bought.

This information never leaves the device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

One-time purchase

The first levels are free. Grown-ups can buy a one-time unlock for the remaining levels. It sits behind a question for grown-ups, so children can't reach it by accident. There is no subscription.

The payment is handled entirely by the store you installed the app from: the App Store on iPhone and iPad, Google Play on Android. The app never sees card, UPI or bank details, and we receive no personal information about the buyer from the app. The store's own terms and privacy policy cover the payment. To get an earlier purchase back on a new device, use "Restore purchase" in the app.

Updates

On Android, the app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, so it can update itself. This check is done by Google Play and the app sends no personal data. On iPhone and iPad, updates come through the App Store.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in or personal details asked for.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No internet access is used by the app itself (only the store, for the optional purchase).
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.
  • No links out of the app.

Children

This app is designed for children. It collects no personal information from children (or anyone else), so nothing is ever shared with us or with third parties.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
